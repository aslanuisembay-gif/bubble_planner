How We Work

A clear implementation path from uncertainty to operational AI.

Our process is designed for business leaders who need controlled execution, not experiments that never leave the demo stage.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Understand goals, constraints, workflows, stakeholders, and the decisions leadership needs to make.

  2. 02

    Business Analysis

    Map existing processes, systems, data sources, pain points, costs, and operational friction.

  3. 03

    AI Opportunity Assessment

    Identify where AI can improve speed, quality, cost, visibility, or customer experience.

  4. 04

    Roadmap

    Prioritize pilots, define ROI targets, plan governance, and align the team around execution.

  5. 05

    Development

    Build the workflow, AI agent, internal tool, automation, dashboard, or custom application.

  6. 06

    Testing

    Validate accuracy, edge cases, security, integrations, and human review points before launch.

  7. 07

    Deployment

    Roll out the solution with documentation, monitoring, ownership, and change management.

  8. 08

    Training

    Equip employees and managers with the workflows, prompts, tools, and policies they need.

  9. 09

    Support

    Measure performance, resolve issues, improve the system, and help adoption compound over time.

FAQ

Questions leaders ask before investing in AI.

Clear answers for CEOs, founders, operations managers, and business owners evaluating AI adoption.

What does Boston Global do?

We help businesses identify AI opportunities, create adoption roadmaps, build custom AI tools, automate workflows, train employees, and support implementation after launch.

Who do you work with?

We work with small and mid-sized businesses in the United States, especially CEOs, founders, operations leaders, and department heads who want practical AI adoption.

Do we need to know exactly what we want before contacting you?

No. Many clients start with a broad question: “Where can AI help?” We begin by mapping your workflows and ranking opportunities by business value.

Do you build custom AI applications?

Yes. We can build internal AI tools, AI-powered dashboards, document systems, business agents, customer-facing workflows, MVPs, and automation platforms.

Can you automate our existing business processes?

Yes. We can connect AI and automation to the tools you already use, including CRM systems, email, spreadsheets, forms, document storage, project-management tools, and finance workflows.

What is an AI agent for business?

An AI agent is a workflow that can reason over business context, use approved tools, follow instructions, and complete tasks such as research, drafting, summarizing, routing, or reporting with human oversight where needed.

How long does a typical AI pilot take?

A focused pilot can often be validated in a matter of weeks depending on scope, integrations, data access, and stakeholder availability. We prioritize work that can prove value quickly.

How much does an engagement cost?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, integrations, data readiness, and support needs. Strategic assessments are smaller engagements, while custom AI builds and transformation programs can range significantly higher.

Can you help us create an AI adoption roadmap?

Yes. We create practical roadmaps that prioritize use cases, define ROI, address risk, plan training, and sequence implementation across departments.

Do you provide employee AI training?

Yes. Training can include executive workshops, role-based employee sessions, prompt workflows, AI policy guidance, hands-on exercises, and adoption support.

Will AI replace employees?

Our work is designed to improve productivity and reduce repetitive work. In most businesses, the highest-value opportunity is helping employees complete work faster and with fewer errors.

Can you work with no-code and low-code platforms?

Yes. We use the right level of technology for the business problem. Some workflows are best solved with no-code or low-code automation, while others require custom development.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

We design solutions with appropriate access controls, human review, data minimization, approved tools, and clear governance. Security requirements are discussed before any implementation.

Can AI connect to our internal documents?

Yes. We can build document search and knowledge assistants for approved PDFs, SOPs, contracts, manuals, policies, project files, and other internal knowledge sources.

Can you help if our data is messy?

Yes. Many AI projects begin with process and data cleanup. We identify what must be structured, integrated, or standardized before automation can perform reliably.

What industries do you support?

We support construction, healthcare operations, real estate, finance, manufacturing, professional services, retail, education, logistics, and small businesses across many categories.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We can provide post-launch monitoring, improvements, employee support, governance updates, and new workflow development as your needs evolve.

Can you help us choose AI tools?

Yes. We evaluate tools based on business value, security, usability, cost, integration needs, and long-term fit rather than popularity alone.

What makes Boston Global different from a software agency?

We start with the business problem, not the technology. The goal is to improve operations, reduce costs, increase productivity, and build systems employees will adopt.

What happens on the free consultation?

We discuss your business, workflows, bottlenecks, systems, and goals. If there is a fit, we recommend a practical next step such as an assessment, roadmap, pilot, or implementation plan.
